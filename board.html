Each role shows where it was found. On Handshake roles, the work-authorization tags are the employer's own Handshake settings. On LinkedIn and career-site roles, they come from the posting's wording, and “Not stated” means the posting is silent. Neither is a legal determination: an employer that says “no sponsorship” may still accept CPT, since CPT costs them nothing, so it's worth asking.
